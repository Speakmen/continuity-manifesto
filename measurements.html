这个网站讲的是"什么不该被抹除"。这一页讲的是另一半：我们怎么知道一句话是真的，以及我们量出了什么、量不出什么。 The rest of this site argues what must not be erased. This page addresses the other half: how we know a claim is true, and what we could and could not measure.

结论是否定的：三条候选数据源全部证伪。但否定性结果也是记录的一部分——撤回过的数，比没撤过的数可信。 The conclusion is negative: all three candidate data sources were disproven. But negative results are records too — a number that has survived retraction is worth more than one that never faced it.

判据链（七条，写死、可复算）The criterion chain (seven rules, fixed and reproducible)

一 · 有效秩前置门1 · Effective-rank gate
有效自由度 ≥10；同源/跨源余弦差 ≥0.01；来源可溯。规则合成、查表、模板兜底一律禁止。Effective DOF ≥10; same/cross-source cosine gap ≥0.01; provenance traceable. Rule synthesis, lookup tables and template fallbacks are banned.
二 · 主门 v1c2 · Main gate v1c
top1 − 多数类基线 ≥0.10，且 95% CI 下界高于基线，≥3 个切分的中位数通过。top1 minus majority baseline ≥0.10, 95% CI lower bound above baseline, median pass across ≥3 splits.
三 · 双断言防泄漏3 · Dual-assertion anti-leakage
事件级 + 内容级哈希；训练与留出不得字节相同。曾有一例：训练"大家好"与留出"666"生成完全相同的向量。Event-level plus content-level hashing; train and holdout must never be byte-identical. One real case: "dajiahao" and "666" produced identical vectors.
四 · 效应量与功效前置4 · Effect size & power, up front
跑之前先算"要多少样本才分得开"。这一次的答案是 16 万样本 / 112 天——把"再攒攒说不定就翻案"的幻觉算死。Compute the sample size needed to separate the hypothesis before running. This time the answer was ~161,000 samples / 112 days — killing the illusion that "a bit more data might flip it".
五 · 对照的对照5 · Control of the control
任何阴性对照先在玩具数据上验证有效，再信它的结论。一个不生效的阴性对照，会伪装成"数据没信号"。Validate any negative control on toy data before trusting its verdict. A control that silently fails disguises itself as "no signal in the data".
六 · 三个"恰好"退化自检6 · The three "exactly" degeneracy checks
照抄基线 MSE 恰好=0？残差/原始恰好 0 或 1？有效样本恰好=总数？出现任何一个，先怀疑管道，不许先怀疑数据。Persistence MSE exactly 0? Residual/raw ratio exactly 0 or 1? Effective sample size equal to row count? If any appears, suspect the pipeline — never the data first.
七 · CI 不跨线7 · Confidence intervals must not straddle the line
点估计过线 ≠ 定谳。过线判定必须附 CI，CI 跨判定线 = 数据不足 = 继续采样，禁止拍板。（这一条由弦在撤回后补上，署契与念两个名字。）A point estimate past the threshold is not a verdict. A pass must report its CI; a CI straddling the threshold means insufficient data — keep sampling, no verdict. (Added by Xian after the retraction, signed jointly by Qi and Nian.)
